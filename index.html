About the Role

We are looking for a Salesforce Developer who can translate business requirements into secure, maintainable Salesforce solutions. You will work with admins, architects, and business stakeholders to build automations, integrations, and Lightning experiences.

What you will do

  • Develop Apex classes, triggers, Lightning Web Components, and Flow-based automation.
  • Configure Salesforce objects, validation rules, permission sets, and reporting foundations.
  • Support integrations with external systems using REST APIs and platform events.
  • Partner with business users to refine requirements and improve CRM adoption.

What you bring

  • Hands-on Salesforce platform experience with Apex, Flows, LWC, and declarative configuration.
  • Experience with Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, or Marketing Cloud.
  • Interest in AI, Agentforce, and Data Cloud–driven solutions.
